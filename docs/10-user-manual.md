# 10 · User manual

## Students

**Register.** On the login page choose *New registration*. Use your university email and roll number, and choose hosteller or day scholar and your diet. Your account stays *pending* until the mess office approves it. You can log in and read the menu meanwhile.

**Subscribe.** *Subscription* tab → pick a plan and the month → *Subscribe & pay* → pay. Joining in the middle of a month? You pay only for the days left. Your subscription is active as soon as the payment is verified. No screenshot needed.

**Eat.** Open *My pass* at the counter. The QR code changes every 30 seconds (watch the bar), so keep the app open rather than taking a screenshot: screenshots are refused. If the camera can't read it, staff can type the code shown under the QR.

**Can't make it?** *Skip / late plate* tab. *Skip* a meal at least 4 hours before it starts (the mess office can change this) and part of its cost goes to your wallet. Changed your mind? *Undo* before the cut-off. Running late? Press *Late* and the mess keeps your plate for up to an hour after the counter closes.

**Single meals.** *Meal tokens* tab → choose date, meal and diet → pay by UPI/card or from your wallet. Tokens can be bought up to 7 days ahead.

**Wallet.** Shows credits from skips and cancellations and what you spent on tokens.

**Cancel.** In *Subscription*, *Request cancellation*. When the mess office approves, you keep today's meals and a share of the unused days is refunded to your wallet.

**Feedback.** Rate any meal you ate (1–5 stars). Raise a complaint and follow its status; you will see the resolution note when it is closed. Vote in committee polls under *Menu & polls* (one vote per poll).

## Committee members
Open *Committee ↗* from the student page.
- **Polls:** write the question, one option per line, a closing time → *Publish*. Close early if needed.
- **Menu:** choose day, meal and diet; the current items appear; edit and *Save slot*.
- **Ratings & complaints:** average stars per meal, the lowest-rated meals of the last 30 days, and complaints the manager escalated to you. Take them up or resolve them with a note.

## Counter staff
1. Log in. The scanner opens with the current meal selected (change it if needed).
2. *Start camera* and point it at the student's QR, or paste/type the code and press Enter.
3. **Green SERVE**: hand over the plate. Check the diet line: **VEG ONLY** or **NON-VEG OK**.
4. **Red DO NOT SERVE**: read the reason (already served at hh:mm, expired pass, no subscription, meal skipped, outside hours). Ask the student to refresh the app if the pass expired.
5. The headcount line shows how many have been served against the expected number.

The camera needs HTTPS (or `localhost`). If the browser blocks it, use the code box.

## Mess office (manager / admin)
| Tab | Use it to |
|---|---|
| Today | See plates to prepare per meal (veg / non-veg) for any date |
| Registrations | Approve or reject new students; search students; disable; add/remove committee members |
| Cancellations | Approve (refund goes to the wallet automatically) or reject |
| Complaints | Mark in progress, escalate to the committee, or resolve with a note |
| Reports | Monthly revenue, subscriptions, meals served, skips, complaints, refused scans; fraud signals; download the meal log for Excel |
| Prices & rules | Plan prices, activate/deactivate plans, token prices, skip cut-off, credit %, refund %, skip limit, late-plate grace |
| Staff & committee | Create counter-staff accounts (admins can also create managers) |
| Audit log | Who changed what and when |
