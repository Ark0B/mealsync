# Student survey (Google Form — copy these questions)

Target: ≥ 50 responses across hostellers and day scholars. Anonymous. Takes ~3 minutes.

1. You are a: Hosteller / Day scholar
2. Your current mess plan: Veg monthly / Non-veg monthly / Pay per meal / None
3. In a typical week, how many mess meals that you have paid for do you **miss**? 0 / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6–10 / more than 10
4. Which meals do you miss most? (multi) Breakfast / Lunch / Snacks / Dinner
5. Have you ever reached the mess and found food finished? Never / Once or twice / Often
6. How often are you asked to show your mess card? Every meal / Sometimes / Rarely / Never
7. Have you seen or heard of someone eating without a valid card? Yes / No / Prefer not to say
8. How do you currently prove payment? Screenshot / Receipt / Cash / Other
9. Would you skip meals in advance if you got partial credit back? Definitely / Probably / Not sure / No
10. How many hours before a meal could you realistically decide to skip it? 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 24
11. Have you ever used the complaint register? Yes / No / Didn't know it existed
12. Do you take part in mess committee WhatsApp polls? Always / Sometimes / Never
13. Would you be comfortable showing a QR code on your phone at the counter? Yes / No (why?)
14. Do you always carry a smartphone with charge to meals? Always / Usually / Not always
15. The single biggest problem with the mess system is: _______
16. One feature you would want in a mess app: _______

**Analysis plan:** Q3 × plan price → money lost per month (the business case for skips); Q10 → default skip cut-off; Q14 → need for the manual-code / printed fallback; Q13 + Q15 → adoption risks.
