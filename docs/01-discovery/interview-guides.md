# Stakeholder interview guides

Record date, interviewer, and notes for each interview in `findings.md`. Ask permission before recording.

## Mess manager (owner: Ananya, with Arkodyuti)
1. Walk me through a new student's registration, start to finish. Where does it get stuck?
2. How do you track who has paid for which month? Can we see the register/Excel format (blank)?
3. How are cancellations and refunds handled today? What is the refund policy?
4. Roughly how many students eat at each meal? How does the kitchen decide how much to cook?
5. What kinds of cheating have you seen? How often?
6. What reports does the university ask you for?
7. What would make you stop using a new system after one week?
8. Who else must approve a change like this (warden, contractor, finance)?

## Counter staff (owner: Sania)
1. During peak lunch, how many students per minute pass the counter?
2. When do you ask for cards? What happens if a student has forgotten it?
3. How do you tell veg-plan students from non-veg?
4. Would scanning a phone slow you down? What would a good screen show you?
5. Is there Wi-Fi / mobile signal at the counter?

## Mess committee (owner: Tarun)
1. How are menu changes decided today? How do you record outcomes?
2. Which complaints come up most? How do they reach you?
3. How do you stop non-members or the same person voting twice in polls?
4. What data would make the general body meeting more productive?

## Day scholars (owner: Ananya)
1. Which meals do you actually eat on campus? Would a lunch-only or lunch+snacks plan fit?
2. How do you pay per meal today?

## Other universities (optional, owner: Arkodyuti)
1. How does your mess verify students? Do you use any software?
2. What would stop your institution from adopting a tool like this?
